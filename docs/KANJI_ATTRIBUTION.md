`public/icons/icon.svg` are generated from **Yuji Boku** (a brush-regular
Japanese typeface).

Yuji Boku is a hand-brushed regular script: thinner and less geometric than a
black Mincho, with visible brush entry/exit shapes. It is **not** a proprietary
欧体/Ouyang Xun font; the best-known Ou-style fonts are commercial and are not
bundled. The design intent is a sharp, handwritten regular script appropriate
for the Bureau's red seal.

If the mark is replaced, remove these paths and this attribution.
